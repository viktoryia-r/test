# faq

<details>

<summary>How often does Beacon refresh data?</summary>

Google Sheets every 15 minutes by default, PostgreSQL live with a five-minute cache, Stripe in real time.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I export a dashboard?</summary>

Yes. Click **Share → Download PDF** to export the current view.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is there a free plan?</summary>

Beacon has a free plan for one workspace with up to three sources. Paid plans remove the limit and add scheduled reports.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Where is my data stored?</summary>

Cloud workspaces are hosted in the EU. Self-hosted instances store everything on your own servers.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I connect more than one Google account?</summary>

Yes. Each Google Sheets source can use a different Google account.

</details>
