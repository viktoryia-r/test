# glossary

**Source** — A connection to an external system such as a Google Sheet, a PostgreSQL database, or Stripe.

**Tile** — A single chart, number, or table on a dashboard.

**Dashboard** — A collection of tiles that share a layout and can be scheduled or shared.

**Alert** — A rule attached to a tile that triggers a webhook or a Slack message when a condition is met.

**Workspace** — The container for sources, dashboards, and members. Each workspace has its own billing.

**Schedule** — A recurring delivery of a dashboard snapshot to email or Slack.
